About Us

Pat Collections Mortgage Bank Limited (also known as Pat Collections Real Estate Finance Co) is a licensed financial services institution dedicated to making property ownership accessible to Nigerians. Led by experienced capital markets professionals, we combine deep financial expertise with a genuine understanding of the Nigerian real estate market. Our mission is to provide transparent, efficient, and customer-focused mortgage banking services that empower individuals and families to achieve their homeownership goals. We are committed to upholding the highest standards of financial integrity, regulatory compliance, and customer service excellence.
